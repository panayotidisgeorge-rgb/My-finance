SpendPattern – Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

In short

SpendPattern keeps your financial data on your phone. We do not run a server for your data, we have no user accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your transactions or personal information.

What the app processes

  • Bank and card statements you import (CSV, Excel, PDF, OFX and similar), the categories you assign, budgets, goals and your settings. All of this is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.
  • Backups you create. When you use "Save preferences" or an export, the file is saved where you choose. Please keep it somewhere safe, because it contains your financial data.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking of your use of the app.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or SMS.

App lock

If you turn on "App lock", the app asks Android to confirm your identity (fingerprint, face or your phone's PIN, pattern or password). Android performs this check. SpendPattern never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data or PIN.

Reading Excel files

To read Excel (.xlsx) files, the app may download a standard open-source library (SheetJS) from a public content delivery network (cdnjs, operated by Cloudflare). Your file is processed on your device and is not uploaded. Like any web request, this download reveals your IP address to that provider.

Subscriptions and purchases

If you subscribe, the purchase is handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment details and subscription status under its own privacy policy. We receive only the information Google provides about whether a subscription is active; we do not receive your payment details.

Your data and your control

Because your data is stored only on your device, you control it. You can delete all data at any time in the app (menu → Remove all data) or by uninstalling the app. Back up your data before changing or resetting your phone: we cannot recover it for you, because we never receive it.

Children

SpendPattern is a personal finance tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Not financial advice

SpendPattern is an informational tool. It does not provide financial, tax or legal advice, and automatic categorisations may be wrong. Please check important figures yourself.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
